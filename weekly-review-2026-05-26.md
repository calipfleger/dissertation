## Ch4 additions

### herrerabecerril2025omz ✓
**Herrera-Becerril, Colas & Sanchez-Cabeza (2025).** Seasonal and interannual variability of the oxygen minimum zone in the Gulf of California entrance: Insights from high-resolution coupled physical-biogeochemical modelling. *Regional Studies in Marine Science.* DOI: 10.1016/j.rsma.2025.104399.
- El Niño → OMZ deepening + contraction; La Niña → OMZ shoaling + expansion
- Mechanism: equatorially forced coastal-trapped waves modulating mesoscale dynamics
- ✓ Directly relevant to Ch4 ENSO–biogeochem–fish habitat linkages

### delteil2026omz ⚠ relevance uncertain
**Delteil, Lévy & Bopp (2026).** Emerging Climate Signals in Tropical Oxygen Minimum Zones. *Biogeosciences* 23, 2205. DOI: 10.5194/bg-23-2205-2026.
- Tropical OMZ edges consistently expanding; northern tropical cores expanding, southern contracting (increased ventilation)
- Regional asymmetry complicates simple "OMZ expands → fish lose habitat" narrative
- ⚠ Background context for Ch4; not focused on ENSO or fisheries directly

### ditkovsky2026migration ⚠ relevance uncertain
**Ditkovsky, Resplandy & Dalaut (2026).** Sensitivity of fish diel vertical migration depths to future changes in the Pacific Ocean oxygen minimum zone. *Frontiers in Marine Science.* DOI: 10.3389/fmars.2025.1716557.
- Fish migration depths shift ±100 m by 2100; OMZ core stable (compensating T/O₂), flanks most sensitive
- CMIP6 ensemble → large geographic uncertainty
- ⚠ Future projections; useful for Ch4 fish-habitat sensitivity framing but not historical ENSO focus
