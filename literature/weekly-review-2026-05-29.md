## Ch2 — VOLC ENSO

### bednarz2026hunga ✓
**Bednarz, E.M. et al. (2026).** Indirect climate impacts of the Hunga eruption. *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics*, 26, 197–215. doi:[10.5194/acp-26-197-2026](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-26-197-2026)

**Relevance:** The 2022 Hunga Tonga eruption introduced anomalously large stratospheric water vapor rather than sulfate aerosol. Using CESM2(WACCM6) large ensembles, Bednarz et al. find statistically significant extratropical climate responses partially mediated by ENSO — extending volcanic ENSO preconditioning literature to a novel eruption regime. Directly relevant to Ch2 discussion of forcing mechanism diversity (aerosol vs. water vapor) and ensemble spread in post-eruption ENSO responses.
