## Ch2 — VOLC ENSO

### king2022reappraisal ✓
**King, J. et al. (2022). A re-appraisal of the ENSO response to volcanism with paleoclimate data assimilation. *Nature Communications*, 13, 747. doi:[10.1038/s41467-022-28210-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28210-1)**

Uses the Last Millennium Reanalysis 2.1 (LMR2.1) paleoclimate data assimilation framework to integrate tree-ring and coral proxies into a millennium-scale ENSO reconstruction. Finds only a **weak** statistical association between volcanism and ENSO; volcanic event selection is the key variable. Spatiotemporal forcing heterogeneity and ENSO preconditioning both modulate the response. Directly relevant to Ch2's ensemble spread interpretation and the proxy network used to validate the iCESM SEA results.
